Customer care

Shipping & returns

Last updated September 27, 2026

Shipping

We ship within Canada only, for a flat rate of $4 per order.

Orders are packed by hand in Vancouver and shipped within 1–3 business days. Delivery usually takes 2–7 business days, depending on where you are in Canada.

If your order hasn't arrived within 15 business days, email us at hello.wordmark@gmail.com and we'll track it down or send a replacement.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your Word‑Mark™ arrives damaged or defective, or we sent the wrong item, email us within 14 days of delivery with your order details and a photo. We'll send a free replacement or give you a full refund, including shipping. You won't need to send anything back.

Returns

Changed your mind? You can return unopened items within 14 days of delivery.

Opening the plastic sleeve means tearing it away from the paper packaging, so once the sleeve has been opened, the item counts as used and can't be returned.

  • Email us at hello.wordmark@gmail.com before sending anything, and we'll give you the return address.
  • You pay for return postage. We recommend a tracked service, as we can't refund items that don't reach us.

Once we receive your return, we'll refund the item price to your original payment method within 5 business days. The original $4 shipping charge isn't refunded. Your bank may take a few more days to show the refund.

Questions

Email us at hello.wordmark@gmail.com. We reply within two business days.
